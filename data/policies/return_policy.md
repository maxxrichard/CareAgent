# Return & Refund Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. The return window starts on the day the carrier marks the parcel as delivered, not on the order date.

## Condition of returned items
Items must be unused, in their original packaging and with all tags attached. Clothing must not have been worn or washed. Electronics must include all accessories, cables and manuals that were in the box.

## Items that cannot be returned
For hygiene and safety reasons the following items are excluded from returns: opened beauty and personal-care products, perishable goods such as food and plants, personalised or engraved items, and gift cards. Sealed items in these categories can still be returned within 30 days if the seal is intact.

## How to start a return
Open the order in your account, select "Return items" and choose a reason. We will email you a prepaid return label within 24 hours. Pack the item securely, attach the label and drop the parcel at any partner drop-off point. You do not need to print anything if you select the QR-code option.

## Return shipping costs
Returns within Germany are free of charge. Returns from other EU countries cost a flat fee of 4.90 EUR, which is deducted from your refund. Returns from outside the EU are at the customer's expense.

## Refund timing
Once the warehouse receives your return, inspection takes up to 3 business days. The refund is then issued to the original payment method. Credit and debit card refunds appear within 5 to 10 business days; PayPal refunds appear within 2 business days; bank transfer refunds take up to 7 business days.

## Partial refunds
If an item is returned damaged, used or incomplete, we may issue a partial refund of up to 50% of the item price. You will receive an email explaining the deduction before the refund is processed.

## Exchanges
We do not offer direct exchanges. To exchange an item, return it for a refund and place a new order for the size or colour you want.
