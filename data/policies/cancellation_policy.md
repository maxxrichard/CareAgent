# Order Cancellation & Changes

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched. Open the order in your account and select "Cancel order". Once an order has been dispatched it can no longer be cancelled; instead, refuse the delivery or return the item once it arrives.

## Changing items in an order
Items in an order cannot be changed after the order is placed. To change items, cancel the order before dispatch and place a new one.

## Refunds for cancelled orders
Cancelled orders are refunded to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. If you paid with a voucher, the voucher value is restored to your account immediately.

## Pre-orders
Pre-ordered items can be cancelled at any time up to the release date. Payment for pre-orders is only taken when the item ships.
