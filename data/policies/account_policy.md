# Account, Privacy & Customer Service

## Contact hours
Our customer-service team is available Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 20:00 and Saturday from 09:00 to 16:00 (Central European Time). Outside these hours you can leave a message and we reply within 1 business day.

## Account deletion
You can request deletion of your account from the privacy settings page. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Order data that we must keep for tax purposes is retained for 10 years in anonymised form.

## Data requests
You can download a copy of all data we hold about you from the privacy settings page. The export is prepared within 72 hours.

## Complaints
If you are unhappy with how a request was handled, ask for the case to be reviewed by a senior agent. Reviews are completed within 5 business days.

## Warranty
All products carry the statutory two-year warranty. Warranty claims are handled by our customer-service team and may require the item to be sent to the manufacturer for assessment, which takes up to 4 weeks.
