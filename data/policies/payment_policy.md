# Payment & Invoices

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, SEPA bank transfer, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Invoice payment is available to customers with at least one previously completed order and is due within 14 days of delivery.

## When you are charged
Card and PayPal payments are authorised when the order is placed and captured when the order is dispatched. Bank transfer orders are dispatched after the payment has been received, which usually takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Invoices
An invoice is emailed to you when the order is dispatched and is always available as a PDF in the order details in your account.

## Vouchers and promotional codes
Only one promotional code can be used per order. Vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash. If you return an order that used a voucher, the voucher value is restored to your account and the remaining amount is refunded to the payment method used.

## Failed or declined payments
If a payment is declined, the order is held for 48 hours so you can update the payment method. After 48 hours the order is cancelled automatically.
