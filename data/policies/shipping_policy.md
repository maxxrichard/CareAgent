# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Delivery options and times
Standard delivery within Germany takes 2 to 4 business days and costs 3.99 EUR. Orders over 49 EUR ship free with standard delivery. Express delivery arrives the next business day when the order is placed before 14:00 and costs 9.99 EUR. Deliveries to other EU countries take 4 to 8 business days.

## Order processing
Orders are processed within 1 business day. Orders placed after 14:00 on a Friday, or on a weekend or public holiday, are processed on the next business day.

## Tracking
As soon as your parcel leaves our warehouse you receive an email with a tracking number. Tracking information is usually visible on the carrier's website within 12 hours of dispatch.

## Delayed deliveries
If your parcel has not arrived 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact us and we will open an investigation with the carrier. Investigations take up to 5 business days. If the parcel cannot be located we will send a replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer.

## Missing or damaged parcels
If a parcel is marked as delivered but you have not received it, please check with neighbours and your building's reception first, then contact us within 7 days of the delivery date. If an item arrives damaged, send us photos of the item and the packaging within 7 days and we will arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed free of charge as long as the order has not been dispatched. After dispatch, address changes must be requested directly with the carrier and are not always possible.

## Failed delivery attempts
The carrier makes two delivery attempts. After the second failed attempt the parcel is held at the nearest pick-up point for 7 days before it is returned to us. Returned parcels are refunded automatically minus the original shipping fee.
